How cases are decided

  • Finance review
    A finance officer endorses the case with an assessment.
  • Independent authorisation
    An authoriser who did not open or review it decides with their signing PIN.
  • Internal waiver
    Reduces the payable amount; the original gross stays on record. On a paid invoice it becomes a refund receivable.
  • Refund
    The paid invoice stays paid; finance records each bank receipt until the approved amount is received.
  • Hold
    While a case is open, an unpaid invoice cannot be approved or settled.
