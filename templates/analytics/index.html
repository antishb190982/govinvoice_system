How to read these figures

  • Billing month: invoices are grouped by service period end, falling back to issue date. Only complete months are included; draft and cancelled invoices are excluded.
  • Billed amount is what providers invoiced. It is not cash spend.
  • Recorded paid / Outstanding now are the current statuses of invoices in the selected billing cohort. They are not an as-of historical reconstruction or a cash-flow statement.
  • Provider comparisons use the equal-length window immediately before the selected period. "New baseline" means there were no prior invoices to compare with.
  • Review prompts need invoices in each of the three months before the ending month, and flag changes of 20% or more against that three-month average. A latest month with no invoices is a data gap, not proof of zero cost. Prompts are for review, not conclusions about cause or fraud.
  • Year on year always compares the rolling 12 months ending {{ history.end|date:"M Y" }} with the preceding 12, whatever period length is chosen. Projections are simple estimates from data up to the ending month; uncertainty is not available.
  • Approval SLA and payment timeliness count events dated within the selected calendar months, filtered by the same unit, provider, utility and currency.
  • Amounts are in {{ currency }} only. Other currencies are never mixed in or converted.
