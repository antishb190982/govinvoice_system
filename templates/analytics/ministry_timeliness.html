How this report is measured

Late receipt means a valid recorded receipt date is after the due date. Late submission means the recorded approval-submission date is after the due date. Recording delays identify invoices entered more than seven days after receipt. Future receipt dates and dates before issue are excluded from receipt-lateness measures. Missing or invalid dates are not assumed timely.

Ministry roll-ups use the invoice's recorded organisational unit, within your access scope. A partial ministry scope is not a whole-ministry total. Dates and surcharges show an association, not proof of who caused a delay or charge. Billed surcharges are already included in invoice totals; this report does not add them again or net off waivers and refunds. Cancelled invoices are excluded.
