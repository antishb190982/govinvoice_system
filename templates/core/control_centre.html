Awaiting and unassigned

"Awaiting" means the record is waiting on a person or an external step (an approver, a provider, a bank file). "Unassigned" means nobody has taken ownership yet; it does not mean the work is stalled by the system. Assign an owner from the record itself.

Bank exceptions are not proof of payment

A bank exception records a mismatch between a statement line and a settlement. Assigning or resolving it does not confirm that funds left the account, and it never changes invoice or payment status.
