File rules

  • CSV, XLS or XLSX, up to 10 MB and 10,000 rows.
  • First worksheet only, first row holds headers. No formulas, no password protection.
  • Required columns: account_number, invoice_number, amount. Optional: currency.
  • One invoice per row, with the full invoice amount, not the account balance.
  • Format identifiers as text so leading zeroes are kept.

How matching works

  • Exact account, invoice number and amount; the currency must equal the statement currency.
  • No exchange-rate conversion, fuzzy matching or tolerance.
  • This statement replaces the previous one for the same provider, unit and currency. Invoices it omits become ineligible, including ones already approved.
  • Mismatches go to the Unsorted basket. Nothing is approved or paid by uploading.
