File format

UTF-8 CSV, up to 10 MB and 10,000 rows. Comma, semicolon or tab separated. One positive debit per row; this is not a full balance or closing statement.

transaction_id
Required. Bank's unique ID. Re-uploading the same ID for this account alias is flagged, never double-counted.
settlement_reference
Required. e.g. SI-2026-000001.
provider_code
Optional. Selects one beneficiary transfer within the settlement. Leave blank to match the settlement total.
amount
Required. Positive, e.g. 1234.50. Fees, credits and reversals become exceptions.
currency
Required. Same as the statement currency.
value_date
Required. YYYY-MM-DD, e.g. 2026-03-31.
bank_reference
Optional. If provided, must match the execution reference recorded on the settlement.
