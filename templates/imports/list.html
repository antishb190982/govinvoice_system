How it works

  • Column names such as Account No, Bill No, Due Date, Total Due are recognised automatically. Providers with unusual layouts get an import template.
  • PDFs, scans and photos are read by OCR (English & French). The analyser locates the provider, account number, dates, net/VAT/total and consumption, with a confidence score and the exact position of each value.
  • Account numbers are matched against registered accounts, and net + VAT = total is cross-checked. Uncertain readings must be confirmed by a person, and each confirmation teaches the provider's template.
  • Select an entire folder (including subfolders), ZIP archives, or mixed files. Supported invoice files are staged; unsupported files are listed. Unsafe archive paths and oversized archives are rejected.
  • Every row is checked: registered account, your unit permissions, duplicates, receipt-date anomalies, and urgency based on payment and approval deadlines.
