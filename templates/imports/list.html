How it works

  • Column names such as Account No, Bill No, Due Date, Total Due are recognised automatically. Providers with unusual layouts get an import template.
  • PDF text is read to find the invoice number, account, dates and total; each PDF becomes the attached original.
  • Every row is checked: registered account, your unit permissions, duplicates against existing invoices and within the file, date logic.
  • Scanned PDFs without a text layer are rejected for manual entry.
